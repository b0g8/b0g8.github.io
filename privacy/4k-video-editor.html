4k Video Editor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

We do not collect personal information. Your projects, the videos and photos you add to them, and the films you export stay on your device. We have no servers that receive your content, and there are no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers in the app.

Your media. The app opens a video or photo only after you have picked it yourself, and only for as long as a project still refers to it. It never asks for access to your whole photo or video library.

Internet. The app goes online only when you ask it to: downloading a music track you chose in the music browser, or opening a media URL you typed.

Donations. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details, and you can cancel a recurring donation at any time in the Play Store.

Crash reports. When the app crashes, or something inside it fails — an export that does not finish, for example — it sends a diagnostic report using Google Firebase Crashlytics. The report holds the error itself, your device model and Android version, and technical details of what the app was doing, such as a video resolution or a number of clips. It holds none of your videos or photos, no file or project names, and no text you typed. We do not attach your name, your account, or any identifier we control. Crashlytics does generate a random installation identifier of its own, so that several reports from one device can be recognised as related; it is not tied to you or to any account, and it is reset if you reinstall the app. We use them only to find and fix faults. Google Play separately collects anonymous crash and performance statistics for apps installed from Play.

No analytics. We do not measure which features you use, how long you spend in the app, or whether you looked at the donation options.

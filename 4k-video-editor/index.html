Requires Android 14 or later. In twelve languages.

What it does

Edit

  • Frame-accurate cuts on a timeline you zoom to a single frame
  • Keyframes: hold a control to animate it
  • Ken Burns zooms and speed ramps in one tap
  • Speed, volume, crop and rotate for every clip

Grade

  • Exposure, contrast, saturation, white balance
  • A colour mixer, curves and a live histogram
  • 15 film looks, or your own .cube LUTs
  • Samsung Log and DJI D-Log M decoded automatically

Effects and words

  • Mirrors, lenses, RGB split, pixelate, toon and more
  • Colour pop with an eyedropper
  • Titles in 69 fonts that fade, slide, pop or wipe
  • Draw on any clip

Sound

  • Voiceover: record over the film or import it, on its own track
  • Search free Creative Commons music, trim and fade it
  • Every clip shows its sound, so the beat is easy to line up

Export

  • 480p to 4K, H.265 or H.264, with no watermark
  • 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 4:3, and nothing is cropped away
  • HDR footage stays HDR with Pro
  • In the background on Android 15 and later

Free, and Pro

  • Everything above is free unless it says Pro
  • Pro adds HDR export, your .cube LUTs and five looks
  • Try them all free in the editor; Pro keeps them in the export
  • Buy once, or subscribe monthly, through Google Play

Privacy

  • It never asks for access to your photo or video library
  • The microphone is used only when you record a voiceover
  • No account, no analytics, no ads

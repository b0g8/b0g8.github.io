4k Video Editor

Rebuilt from the ground up. A timeline you can zoom to a single frame, film looks you see on your own footage before you choose one, and 4K export straight to your gallery.

No ads. No watermark. Nothing behind a subscription.

Get it on Google Play What's coming next

Requires Android 14 or later. The interface is in English.

What it does

Edit

  • A timeline you zoom with two fingers, from whole film to single frames
  • Cut at the playhead and drop what you do not want
  • Speed, volume and crop, adjusted by gesture on the picture itself
  • Undo anything

Look

  • Film looks applied to your own footage before you choose one
  • Colour and effect filters, previewed live rather than guessed at
  • What the preview shows is what the export gives you — the same shader runs in both

Sound and words

  • A music track: trim it, place it, leave silence where you want silence
  • Text and captions, placed on the frame
  • Draw on any clip

Shape

  • Widescreen, vertical, square, 4:5 or 4:3 — chosen before you edit, not at export
  • Nothing you shot is ever cropped away by a change of shape
  • The app turns with your film: pick a vertical shape and it stays vertical

Export

  • 480p, 720p, 1080p or 4K, straight to your gallery
  • HDR footage stays HDR when your film can keep it
  • No watermark — there has never been one and there will not be one

Privacy

  • It never asks for access to your photo or video library
  • It opens only the files you pick yourself
  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads

Supporting it

The app is free and complete. If it is useful to you, there is a donation behind the coin in the project menu — one-time or monthly, entirely optional. It unlocks nothing, because there is nothing locked.

What gets built next is a separate question, and the roadmap is where it is answered.

Something exported wrong?

That is the one report worth more than any other. Audio drifting out of sync, a file that will not play, a colour that is not what the preview showed — send the device model and what you were editing.

Retro Player

An Android music player that looks like it is 1997 and behaves like it is not.

There is nothing to install yet and no date to promise. When there is one, it will be on this page.

What it does

Your music, on your terms

  • Finds the audio on your phone and asks before adding any of it
  • Tick what you want, strike out what you don't
  • It never asks about the same file twice

Sound

  • A real ten-band equaliser, at the classic frequencies
  • The same ten bands on every device, not whatever the phone offers
  • A spectrum analyser driven by the actual audio — and it needs no microphone permission to do it

Radio

  • Paste a Shoutcast, Icecast or HLS URL and it plays
  • Station song titles update as they change

Out of the way

  • Keeps playing in the background
  • Lock screen, Bluetooth and Android Auto controls
  • Three colour categories you name yourself, to filter the playlist down

What it deliberately does not do

No library browser. No recommendations. No account, no analytics, no video. It plays a playlist.

About the look

This is a homage to the desktop players of the late nineties, drawn entirely from scratch: every pixel on screen is produced by code, and no third-party skin file, bitmap or sprite sheet is used anywhere.

It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the makers of the player it is inspired by.
